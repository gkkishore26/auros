About Us

AUROS was born from a simple frustration.

As a student juggling university coursework alongside a passion for video editing, I found myself spending more time searching for the right tutorial than actually creating. Long, rambling videos that took twenty minutes to explain what could be shown in two. Expensive asset packs that promised the world but delivered mediocre results. A creative process that felt more like fighting through a maze than making something beautiful.

So I started making my own presets. First for myself, then for friends who noticed my edits looked different - cleaner, sharper, more professional. What began as a personal collection of color grades, transitions, and motion graphics slowly grew into something bigger.

I built this alongside my degree, often editing between lectures and burning midnight oil to get a new pack just right. Every preset, every template, every asset on this site was crafted with the same philosophy:

Make it easier to start, faster to improve, and simpler to create.

No fluff. No unnecessary complexity. Just tools that work the way you expect them to - so you can spend less time hunting for the right effect and more time making your vision come alive.

AUROS is still just me, still a student, still editing at 2 AM. But if these tools save you even a fraction of the time they've saved me, then this project has done exactly what it set out to do.

Thank you for being here.
